# T2.2 Migrate reservation unit pricings to pricing groups

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.1, T2.7

## Background

Each reservation unit now has a list of `ReservationUnitPricing` rows with a `begins` date.
In the new model, each reservation unit that does not share prices gets its own pricing group.

## Scope

For each reservation unit with pricings:

- Make one PricingGroup with no unit group, named after the reservation unit.
- Make one ReservationUnitPricingGroup with the date of its first pricing.
- For each pricing, make one `BASE` PriceComponent:
  - `valid_from = begins`, `valid_until` = the `begins` of the next pricing (null for the last one)
  - `price_option = null`, `amount = highest_price`, the same `price_unit`, `tax_level`, `payment_type`
    and `material_price_description`
  - `can_apply_for_discount = True` if the reservation unit has `can_apply_free_of_charge=True` and `highest_price > 0`
  - `accounting = null`

Keep `ReservationUnitPricing` until the cleanup (T10.1).

## Acceptance criteria

- Every non-draft reservation unit has a pricing group after the migration.
- For a sample of reservation units and dates, the old and the new calculation give the same price.
- The migration is reversible, or the ticket documents why not.
