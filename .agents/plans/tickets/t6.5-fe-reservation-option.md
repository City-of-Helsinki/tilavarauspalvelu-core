# T6.5 Price option selection in the reservation flow

Label: FE
Blocked by: T6.2, T6.4

## Background

The reserver selects a price option, for example "Adults", and the price depends on it. The backend stores the option (T6.2).

## Scope

- Show the offered options from the price query.
- Send the selected option when the reservation is made or changed.
- Show the price of the selected option.

## Acceptance criteria

- The flow follows the design from T6.4.
