# T8.7 Elapsed time condition

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.3, T2.5

## Background

Price components have condition columns. The validator rejects a value in a column until the price calculation supports it.
Some prices are graduated, for example a land area: the first day 276 €, each extra day 138 €.

## Scope

- Support `elapsed_from` and `elapsed_to`, measured from the reservation start. The calculation cuts the reservation at these points.
- Turn the condition on in the validator and in the coverage check (T2.3).
- The batch API (T2.4) and the price preview (T9.2) accept the condition.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reservation of three days costs 276 € + 2 × 138 €.
- Tests cover the boundaries of the condition.
