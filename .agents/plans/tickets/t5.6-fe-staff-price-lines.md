# T5.6 Price lines and override in the staff reservation view

Label: FE
Blocked by: T5.4, T5.5

## Background

Reservations have price lines, and the handler can set an override per line.

## Scope

- Show the price lines in the staff reservation view.
- Let the handler set the override per line in the approve flow.

## Acceptance criteria

- The view follows the design from T5.5.
