# T8.2 Surcharge components

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.5, T5.1

## Background

A surcharge adds to the rent, in €. Example: the evening supervision fee of school gyms, added to the hourly rent.
Surcharges can overlap with each other and with base components. Each surcharge gets its own price line,
with its own VAT and accounting.

## Scope

- Turn on `kind=SURCHARGE` in the validator.
- In the calculation, add every matching surcharge to each piece of the reservation.
- `is_adjustable` says if adjustments (T8.3) change the surcharge. Staff choose it.
- Each surcharge component gets its own price line.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reservation with a matching surcharge has two price lines: rent and surcharge.
- Two matching surcharges both apply.
