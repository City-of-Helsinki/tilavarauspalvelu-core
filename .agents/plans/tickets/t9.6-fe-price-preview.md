# T9.6 Price preview in the pricing group editor

Label: FE
Blocked by: T9.2, T9.4

## Background

The backend gives a price preview with unsaved changes (T9.2).

## Scope

- In the editor, let staff select a price option, a date and a time range, and show the price lines.
- The preview uses the unsaved changes of the editor.

## Acceptance criteria

- The preview follows the design from T9.3.
