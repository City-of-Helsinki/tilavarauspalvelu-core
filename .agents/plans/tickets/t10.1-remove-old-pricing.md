# T10.1 Remove the old pricing models and fields

Label: BE
Blocked by: T3.1, T3.2, T5.2, T7.1, T10.2

## Background

After the redesign, the old pricing data is no longer read or written.

## Scope

- Remove `ReservationUnitPricing` and `TaxPercentage`.
- Remove `ReservationUnit.payment_product` and `ReservationUnit.can_apply_free_of_charge`.
- Remove `Reservation.tax_percentage_value` and `Reservation.unit_price`.
- Remove their admin views, GraphQL fields and test factories.
- Keep `ReservationStatistic` fields that copy these values, or fill them from the price lines.

## Acceptance criteria

- No code reads or writes the removed models and fields.
