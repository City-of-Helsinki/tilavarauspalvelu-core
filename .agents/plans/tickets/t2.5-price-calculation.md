# T2.5 Price calculation with pricing groups

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.2

## Background

The price is now calculated from `ReservationUnitPricing` in several places: reservation create, confirm, approve,
reschedule and payment. All of these must use the new pricing groups.

## Scope

Calculation inputs: reservation unit, price option (nullable), begin, end and creation time.

1. Find the active pricing group by the reservation start date.
2. Keep the components where `price_option` is null or equal to the selected option.
3. Keep the components whose whole-reservation conditions match: total duration and lead time.
4. Cut the reservation into pieces at local midnight, at `valid_from` and `valid_until`, and at every condition boundary.
5. For each piece, find the one base component that matches. If there is none, the reservation is not allowed.
6. Add the pieces together per component. A `FIXED` component counts once. Other units count the duration of the
   matching pieces. Round the total duration up to the next 15 minutes. Units of a day or more count each started unit.

This ticket supports only base components with no conditions, and the `FIXED` and per-time price units.
Group 8 adds the rest.

- Replace all uses of `get_active_pricing` with the new calculation.
- A non-draft reservation unit must have a pricing group that begins on or before today. Otherwise it is not reservable.
- The payment type of a reservation is the `payment_type` of the base component that matches its start.

## Acceptance criteria

- All existing price tests pass with the new calculation.
- A reservation over a price change date gets the correct mix of the two prices.
