# T3.3 Update the reservation unit pricing form design

Label: DES
Blocked by: none

## Background

The pricing form of a reservation unit has a current and a future price. The backend changes some fields:

- No separate lowest and highest price. A reservation unit has one price. The handler can make a reservation free.
- No net price fields. The VAT comes from a tax level, and the rate can change over time.
- No "activated on begins" checkbox. VAT changes are handled by tax levels.
- "Can apply for free of charge" is set per pricing, so a change to it can be scheduled.

The reservation unit form also gets a choice: "own pricing" (this form) or "shared pricing group" (see T9.3).

## Scope

- Update the form design for these changes.
- Keep the current form as close to its current layout as possible.

## Acceptance criteria

- A design that the frontend team can implement in T3.4.
