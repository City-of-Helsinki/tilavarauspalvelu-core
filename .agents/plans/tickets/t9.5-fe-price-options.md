# T9.5 Price option management

Label: FE
Blocked by: T6.1, T9.3

## Background

Price options belong to a unit group. Staff manage them together with pricing groups.

## Scope

- List, create and edit the price options of the user's unit groups.
- Turn an option off with `is_active`.

## Acceptance criteria

- The view follows the design from T9.3.
