# T8.5 Time of day condition

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.3, T2.5

## Background

Price components have condition columns. The validator rejects a value in a column until the price calculation supports it.
Some prices differ by the time of day, for example an evening supervision fee in school gyms.

## Scope

- Support `time_begins` and `time_ends`. The calculation cuts the reservation at these times. A window over midnight needs two components.
- Turn the condition on in the validator and in the coverage check (T2.3).
- The batch API (T2.4) and the price preview (T9.2) accept the condition.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reservation from 16:00 to 19:00 with an evening price from 17:00 gets two prices.
- Tests cover the boundaries of the condition.
