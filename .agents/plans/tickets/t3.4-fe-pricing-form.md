# T3.4 Implement the updated reservation unit pricing form

Label: FE
Blocked by: T1.5, T3.1, T3.2, T3.3

## Background

The backend saves the reservation unit's pricing form to the new pricing model. Some fields change.

## Scope

- Remove the lowest price and the net price fields.
- Move "can apply for free of charge" from the reservation unit into each pricing.
- Keep payment type and the material price description in each pricing.
- Show coverage errors from the backend. They can point to a pricing the user did not change.

## Acceptance criteria

- Staff can create and edit the current and the future price as before.
- The form follows the design from T3.3.
