# T8.1 Total duration brackets

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.3, T2.5

## Background

Price components have condition columns. The validator rejects a value in a column until the price calculation supports it.
Event prices depend on the duration of the whole reservation, for example "6 h or more" and "under 6 h". The price is fixed per event.

## Scope

- Support `min_total_duration` and `max_total_duration`. A component matches if the total duration is in the range. The price unit must be `FIXED`.
- Turn the condition on in the validator and in the coverage check (T2.3).
- The batch API (T2.4) and the price preview (T9.2) accept the condition.

## Acceptance criteria

- An event of 5 h and an event of 7 h get the prices of their brackets.
- Tests cover the boundaries of the condition.
