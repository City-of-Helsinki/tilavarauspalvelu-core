# T3.5 Tell staff about the changed pricing form

Label: OPS
Blocked by: T3.4

## Background

The pricing form of reservation units changes: no lowest price, tax levels instead of percentages,
and "can apply for free of charge" per pricing.

## Scope

- Write a short release note for staff users.
- Update the staff instructions, if they describe the pricing form.

## Acceptance criteria

- Staff get the note before or at the release.
