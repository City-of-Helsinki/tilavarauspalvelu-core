# T7.1 Web shop products per accounting and tax level

Label: BE
Blocked by: T1.3, T5.1, T7.3

## Background

Each reservation unit has one web shop product. The web shop keeps one accounting, with one VAT code, per product.
Price lines of one reservation can have different accountings and VAT rates, so they need different products.

The web shop makes the product ID from `UUID3(UUID3(namespace, namespaceEntityId), merchantId)`.
So one product belongs to exactly one combination of reservation unit, accounting, tax level and merchant.

## Scope

- `PaymentProduct` gets `reservation_unit`, `accounting` and `tax_level`.
  Unique `(reservation_unit, accounting, tax_level, merchant)`.
- Migration: each existing product gets the reservation unit that links to it through `ReservationUnit.payment_product`,
  that unit's accounting, and the tax level of its active pricing.
- `namespaceEntityId` is `"<reservation unit pk>"` for the reservation unit's own accounting and default tax level.
  This keeps the existing product IDs. Other combinations use `"<reservation unit pk>:<accounting pk>:<tax level pk>"`.
- Create the products and their accountings when a reservation unit or its pricing changes.
- Each price line uses the product of its accounting and tax level. A merchant change makes a new product row.
  The old row stays for old orders and refunds.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reservation with lines of two VAT rates is paid with two products in the test web shop.
- Existing product IDs do not change.
