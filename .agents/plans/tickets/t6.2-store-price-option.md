# T6.2 Store the selected price option

Label: BE
Blocked by: T6.1, T4.1

## Background

The reserver selects a price option, and the price depends on it.

## Scope

- `Reservation.price_option` and `ApplicationSection.price_option`, both nullable.
- Reservation create and update take the option and use it in the price calculation.
- The option is required if the reservation unit's pricing group offers options on that date.
- The price query (T4.1) returns the offered options for the selected time.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reservation with an option gets the price of that option.
