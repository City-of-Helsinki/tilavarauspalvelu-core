# T6.4 Design price option selection

Label: DES
Blocked by: none

## Background

A reserver selects a price option, for example "Adults" or "Juniors, seniors and special groups".
The reserver selects it themselves, and nothing checks it automatically.

## Scope

- Option selection in the reservation flow, with the price for each option.
- Option selection in the application form, for each application section.

## Acceptance criteria

- A design that the frontend team can implement in T6.5 and T6.6.
