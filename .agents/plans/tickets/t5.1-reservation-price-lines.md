# T5.1 Save price lines on reservations

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.5

## Background

A reservation has one price and one VAT percentage. With surcharges, one price can have parts with different VAT
and accounting. A price line is one row of the price, and a copy of the values used. Later price changes do not change it.

## Scope

- Model `ReservationPriceLine`:
  - `reservation`, `price_component` (nullable, `SET_NULL`), `description` (translated)
  - `quantity`, `price_unit`, `unit_amount`
  - `amount_before_adjustment`, `adjustment_percentage`, `amount`
  - `override_amount` (nullable, see T5.4)
  - `tax_percentage`, `vat_code`, `accounting`, `payment_product` (nullable)
- Make one line per base or surcharge component when a reservation is made or changed.
- `Reservation.price` is the sum of the final amounts. `Reservation.non_subsidised_price` is the sum without overrides.
- When a web shop order is made, set `tax_percentage` and `vat_code` again from the rate on that day. The amounts do not change.
- The accounting of a line comes from the component, then the reservation unit, then the unit.

## Acceptance criteria

- New reservations have price lines, and their sum is the reservation price.
- A later price change does not change existing lines.
