# T8.8 Lead time condition

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.3, T2.5

## Background

Price components have condition columns. The validator rejects a value in a column until the price calculation supports it.
A price can depend on how early the reservation is made, for example an early booking discount or a last-minute price.

## Scope

- Support `min_lead_time` and `max_lead_time`: the time from the reservation creation to its start. The whole reservation matches or not.
- Turn the condition on in the validator and in the coverage check (T2.3).
- The batch API (T2.4) and the price preview (T9.2) accept the condition.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reservation made 30 days ahead and one made 1 day ahead get different prices.
- Tests cover the boundaries of the condition.
