# T2.6 Check migrated prices in production

Label: OPS
Blocked by: T2.2

## Background

The data migration moves all reservation unit prices to a new model. A wrong price can cause a payment reclamation.

## Scope

- Before the deploy: export the active and future prices of all reservation units.
- After the deploy: compare them with the prices from the new calculation.
- Check a sample of reservation units in the staff UI and the customer UI.

## Acceptance criteria

- No price differs, or each difference is explained and fixed.
