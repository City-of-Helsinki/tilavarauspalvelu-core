# T4.5 Remove price calculation from the customer app

Label: FE
Blocked by: T4.4

## Background

After T4.4, the customer app does not need to calculate prices.

## Scope

- Remove `getPriceString`, `getReservationUnitPrice`, `getPrice`, `getActivePricing`, `getFuturePricing`
  and other helpers that read pricings, and their tests.
- Remove the pricing fields from the customer queries.

## Acceptance criteria

- No customer code reads `lowestPrice`, `highestPrice` or `pricings`.
