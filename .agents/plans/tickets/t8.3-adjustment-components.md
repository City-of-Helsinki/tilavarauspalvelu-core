# T8.3 Adjustment components

Label: BE
Blocked by: T8.2

## Background

An adjustment changes the price by a percentage, for example a summer discount of −20 % or a fee of +10 %.
It has no VAT, no accounting and no price line of its own. It changes the price lines it applies to.

## Scope

- Turn on `kind=ADJUSTMENT` in the validator. `amount` is the percentage and can be negative.
- Calculation for each piece of a reservation:
  1. Add the base and the matching surcharges with `is_adjustable=true`.
  2. Add up the percentages of the matching adjustments. The total cannot go below −100 %. Apply it to the sum of step 1.
  3. Add the matching surcharges with `is_adjustable=false`.
- Fold the adjustment into each line: `amount_before_adjustment`, `adjustment_percentage` and `amount`.
  A separate line is not used, because one adjustment can apply to lines with different VAT rates.
- The handler's override (T5.4) is applied after the adjustments.

## Acceptance criteria

- −20 % and +10 % on the same piece give −10 %.
- A surcharge with `is_adjustable=false` does not change.
- The total adjustment never makes a line negative.
