# T8.6 Recurring date condition

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.3, T2.5

## Background

Price components have condition columns. The validator rejects a value in a column until the price calculation supports it.
Some prices repeat every year in a season, for example a heated field from 15.12. to 15.3.

## Scope

- Support `dates_begin` and `dates_end` as month and day. A range can go over the new year. The calculation cuts the reservation at these dates.
- Turn the condition on in the validator and in the coverage check (T2.3).
- The batch API (T2.4) and the price preview (T9.2) accept the condition.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reservation on 14.12. and one on 16.12. get different prices.
- Tests cover the boundaries of the condition.
