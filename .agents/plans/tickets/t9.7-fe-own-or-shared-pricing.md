# T9.7 "Own pricing" or "shared pricing group" in the reservation unit form

Label: FE
Blocked by: T3.4, T9.1, T9.3

## Background

A reservation unit uses its own pricing (the pricing form, T3.4) or a shared pricing group.
The backend lists the shared groups that the reservation unit can use (T9.1).

## Scope

- Add the choice to the reservation unit form.
- For a shared group: a select of the allowed groups and a start date.
- Show the active and the scheduled pricing group of the reservation unit.

## Acceptance criteria

- The form follows the design from T9.3.
