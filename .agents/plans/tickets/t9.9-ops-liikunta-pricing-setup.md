# T9.9 Set up the sports department's pricing groups

Label: OPS
Blocked by: T9.4, T9.5, T9.7, T9.8, T8.1, T6.5

## Background

The sports department's price list ("Liikuntapaikkavarausten hinnasto 1.6.2026 alkaen") has five price categories.
Each category has hourly prices for three customer groups and event prices for four event groups, adults and juniors,
for 6 h or more and under 6 h.

## Scope

- Create the price options from the price list.
- Create one shared pricing group per price category, with its hourly and event prices.
- Move each sports reservation unit to the pricing group of its category from an agreed date.
- Check a sample of prices with the price preview.

## Acceptance criteria

- The prices in Varaamo match the price list.
