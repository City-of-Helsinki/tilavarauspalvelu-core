# T9.8 Create the sports department unit group

Label: OPS
Blocked by: none

## Background

Shared pricing groups and price options belong to a unit group. The sports department (liikunta) has many units.
A unit group with these units may not exist in production yet.

## Scope

- Check if a unit group for the sports department exists in production.
- If not, create it and add the sports department's units.
- Give the sports department's pricing staff a role on the unit group.

## Acceptance criteria

- The sports department's staff can see the unit group in the pricing views.
