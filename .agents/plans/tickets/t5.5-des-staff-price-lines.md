# T5.5 Design price lines in the staff reservation view

Label: DES
Blocked by: none

## Background

A reservation price now has lines, for example rent and a supervision fee. The handler can set the final amount of each line.

## Scope

- Show the price lines in the staff reservation view.
- Let the handler set the final amount per line, and keep a "free of charge" action for the whole reservation.

## Acceptance criteria

- A design that the frontend team can implement in T5.6.
