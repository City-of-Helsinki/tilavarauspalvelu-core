# T4.3 Design the price display in the customer app

Label: DES
Blocked by: none

## Background

A reservation unit can have several prices at once (price options), time-based prices, surcharges and discounts.
The backend gives a price summary for lists and a full price for a selected time.

## Scope

- Price summary on search cards and the reservation unit page, for example "from 20 €/h".
- Full price in the reservation flow, with price lines when there are several.
- Material price descriptions, for example materials that are paid later.

## Acceptance criteria

- A design that the frontend team can implement in T4.4.
