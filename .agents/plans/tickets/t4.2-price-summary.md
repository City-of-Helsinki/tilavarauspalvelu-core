# T4.2 Price summary for lists and search cards

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.5

## Background

Search cards and lists show a short price, for example "10 €/h" or "10 € – 20 €".
With price options and conditions, a reservation unit can have many prices.

## Scope

- A field on the reservation unit that returns a short summary of the active prices:
  the lowest base price and its price unit, and if the price varies.
- Free reservation units return a "free" summary.

## Acceptance criteria

- The customer app can show "from X €/h" or "free" without calculating prices itself.
