# T1.4 Pick the VAT rate by the date of the sale

Label: BE
Blocked by: T1.2

## Background

By law, the VAT rate follows the date of the sale. The current code already sets the VAT again when a handled
reservation is approved and when a pending reservation is paid. It does this through `is_activated_on_begins`.
With tax levels, the rule must be explicit.

## Scope

- The VAT rate of a reservation is the rate of its tax level on the date of the sale:
  - For a paid reservation, the day the web shop order is made. For a handled reservation, this is the day
    of approval or of a later payment.
  - For a reservation with no web shop order, the day the reservation is made.
- Update the approve serializer and the payment view (`api/rest/views.py`) to use the tax level rate of today.
- The start date of the reservation does not affect the rate.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reservation made before a VAT change and paid after it gets the new rate.
- A free reservation keeps the rate of the day it was made.
- Tests cover the approval and the later payment cases.
