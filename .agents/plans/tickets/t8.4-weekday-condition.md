# T8.4 Weekday condition

Label: BE
Blocked by: T2.3, T2.5

## Background

Price components have condition columns. The validator rejects a value in a column until the price calculation supports it.
Some prices differ on weekdays and at weekends, for example a parking area.

## Scope

- Support `weekdays`. The calculation cuts the reservation at local midnight, and each piece matches the components of its weekday.
- Turn the condition on in the validator and in the coverage check (T2.3).
- The batch API (T2.4) and the price preview (T9.2) accept the condition.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reservation from Friday to Saturday gets the weekday price for Friday and the weekend price for Saturday.
- Tests cover the boundaries of the condition.
